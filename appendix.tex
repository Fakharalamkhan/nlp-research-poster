\section*{Use of Generative AI Tools}
As required by the declaration below, the generative AI tools used for this poster and the
underlying study are listed here with their product names.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Claude Code} (Anthropic), model Claude Opus 5.5: used to prepare and check the code
  repository, to adjust the analysis scripts (rounding of table values, figure layout), and to draft
  the layout, text and charts of this poster and appendix from the author's experimental results.
\end{itemize}

\noindent No generative AI tool was used to produce data, labels or reported numbers. All numbers on
the poster come from the logged experiment outputs in the repository (folder \texttt{experiments/}). All AI-generated content was
reviewed and revised by the author, who takes full responsibility for it.
